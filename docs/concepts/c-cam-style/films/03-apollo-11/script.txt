WOULD YOU SURVIVE LANDING ON THE MOON? (Apollo 11, 1969)

1969. You are an astronaut.
Your lander is famously light. Its walls are famously thin.
Your computer is far weaker than a modern phone.
During the descent, an alarm starts blaring.
It is called a 1202 alarm. Nobody has seen it in training.
Mission control says: keep going.
Then you spot the landing area. It is full of boulders.
So the commander flies the last part by hand.
Reports say fuel is dangerously low.
You land.
One crew member stays in orbit alone, the whole time.
Then you step outside, and come home safely.
Survival tip: stay calm. And trust the person flying.
