WOULD YOU SURVIVE AS A SAMURAI IN PEACEFUL JAPAN? (Edo period, c. 1750)

Japan, 1750. You wake up a samurai.
You expected battles. Glory. Dramatic sword fights.
Instead, Japan has been at peace for generations.
So most samurai worked as clerks and officials for their lord.
You still carry two swords. Proudly. Mostly for show.
Your pay comes in rice. Not coins. Rice.
But rice has to be sold, and prices keep changing.
So you borrow money from a merchant. Many samurai did.
In theory, merchants rank below you.
In practice, they hold your debts.
Etiquette is strict. One wrong bow, and your whole day is ruined.
Survival tip: be very polite to your rice merchant.
