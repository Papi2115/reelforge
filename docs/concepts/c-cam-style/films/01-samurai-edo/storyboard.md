# Samurai in peaceful Japan — storyboard (60 s, English captions, no audio)

FACTS (keep hedged): Edo period 1603–1868 = long peace; many samurai worked as officials/clerks; they wore two swords as a status mark; stipends paid in rice; many samurai ended up in debt to merchants; merchants ranked lower socially. No invented numbers.

CAST (roles; each character individually hand-built per variant):
- YOU: nervous young samurai, secretly wants a duel, mostly fills forms.
- THE MERCHANT: smug, polite, holds your debts, abacus. Recurring gag: he is always one bow lower than you but clearly in charge.
- THE ELDER OFFICIAL: your stern superior, obsessed with etiquette and stamping documents.
- THE CLERK COLLEAGUE: tired, ink-stained, sleeps at his desk.
- Optional: a stray cat that steals your dignity; a few townspeople.

SHOTS (target times, total 60 ± 2 s; hard cuts; camera freedom per variant rules)
1. 0–4 TITLE poster card: "WOULD YOU SURVIVE AS A SAMURAI IN PEACEFUL JAPAN?" — three mains lined up.
2. 4–10 HOUSE, dawn: "Japan, 1750. You wake up a samurai." You on a futon, two swords on a rack, sword gleams, you smile.
3. 10–16 DAYDREAM then DESK: "You expected battles. Glory. Dramatic sword fights." (imagined clash silhouette) → cut to the real desk: stacks of papers.
4. 16–22 OFFICE: "Instead, Japan has been at peace for generations. So most samurai worked as clerks and officials for their lord." Elder stamps, clerk asleep, you stare out the window at a bird.
5. 22–28 STREET: "You still carry two swords. Proudly. Mostly for show." You strut; the swords hit a doorframe/another passer-by's swords, awkward bump gag.
6. 28–34 RICE STORE: "Your pay comes in rice. Not coins. Rice." You receive a sack that is too big; you carry it.
7. 34–40 MARKET: "But rice has to be sold, and prices keep changing." Price board flips; you panic.
8. 40–47 MERCHANT'S SHOP: "So you borrow money from a merchant. Many samurai did." Handshake-free exchange: you bow, he bows lower; coins change hands (hand contact must be accurate).
9. 47–53 SHOP close-up: "In theory, merchants rank below you. In practice, they hold your debts." The merchant's ledger with your name, long list.
10. 53–58 STREET/OFFICE: "Etiquette is strict. One wrong bow, and your whole day is ruined." You over-bow, hat falls, elder frowns.
11. 58–62 PAYOFF: "Survival tip: be very polite to your rice merchant." You bow deeply; he bows slightly; cat sits on your sword.
