WOULD YOU SURVIVE AS A MIAMI GANGSTER IN THE 80s? (A day in the life - satire)

Would you survive as a Miami gangster in the 80s?
Miami, the mid-1980s.
On TV, it's pastel suits, speedboats and neon.
Real life: a shabby South Beach hotel.
Your neighbours are retirees.
Your sports car is borrowed.
It doesn't start.
So you take the bus.
In a white linen suit. In August.
Your boss yells for an hour about money you never even saw.
Banks have to report big cash deposits.
So the cash lives in a freezer.
Your accountant counts it in oven mitts.
Since 1982, a federal task force has been hunting people like you.
So everyone suspects everyone.
Your best friend is wearing a wire.
You try to escape by speedboat.
It runs out of fuel.
The glamour ends in a courtroom, under fluorescent lights.
Survival tip: it never ends well.
Get a boring job. Wear sunscreen.
