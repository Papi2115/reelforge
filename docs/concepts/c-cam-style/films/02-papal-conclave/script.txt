HOW NOT TO CHOOSE A POPE (Viterbo, Italy, 1268)

Viterbo, Italy. 1268. The pope has died.
Now the cardinals must choose a new one.
They argue. For months. Then for years.
Reports say it took nearly three years.
The townspeople lose patience.
First, they lock the cardinals inside.
Still no pope.
So they cut the food. Bread and water.
Still no pope.
Reports say they even removed the roof.
Finally, the cardinals chose Gregory the Tenth.
Later, the rules were changed: electors would be locked in.
The word is conclave. It means: with a key.
Survival tip: when the locals take your roof, vote.
