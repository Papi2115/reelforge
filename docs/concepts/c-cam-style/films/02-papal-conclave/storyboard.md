# How not to choose a pope — storyboard (60 s, English captions, no audio)

FACTS (keep hedged): After Pope Clement IV died in 1268 the cardinals met in Viterbo; the election dragged on for nearly three years (to 1271). Reports say local officials/townspeople locked the cardinals in, cut their food to bread and water, and removed the palace roof. Gregory X was elected; later rules (1274) formalised locking electors in — 'conclave' from Latin 'cum clave', with a key. No invented numbers; say 'reports say'.

CAST (individually hand-built per variant):
- THE STUBBORN CARDINAL (main): huge ego, never agrees with anyone, keeps a tiny notebook of grievances.
- THE TIRED CARDINAL (main): wants to go home, sleeps on his chair, eats the least.
- THE MAYOR / TOWN OFFICIAL (main): practical, impatient, holds a huge key and a plank.
- GREGORY, the chosen cardinal: oblivious, wandered in late, ends up pope (small role).
- A few townspeople (a roofer with a ladder, a baker with a loaf).
