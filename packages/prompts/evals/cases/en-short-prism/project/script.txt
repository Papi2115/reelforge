Here is a quick experiment you can try at home. Take a glass of water, place it on a table, and shine a flashlight through it at an angle. You will see a tiny rainbow on the wall behind it.

That happens because white light is a mix of many colors, and water bends each color by a slightly different amount. Red bends the least, violet bends the most. Isaac Newton described this effect in 1672, using a simple prism and a dark room.
