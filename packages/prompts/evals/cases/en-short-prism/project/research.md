# Research: rainbow in a glass of water

## Key facts

- White light is a mixture of colours; refraction separates them (dispersion) — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dispersion_(optics)
- Water's refractive index is higher for violet than for red light — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refractive_index
- Newton described splitting sunlight with a prism in 1672 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opticks

## Timeline

- 1672: Newton's letter "New Theory about Light and Colours" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton
- 1704: Opticks published — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opticks

## People & entities

- Isaac Newton — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton

## Numbers worth showing on screen

- Refractive index of water about 1.33 — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refractive_index

## Open questions / uncertain
