# Beats

1. Hook (0–5 s): an experiment you can do at home. Turn: curiosity.
2. Do it (5–19 s): glass, table, flashlight, rainbow. Turn: wow.
3. Why (19–30 s): white light is a mix; water bends colours differently (research: Key facts). Turn: understanding.
4. Payoff (30–37 s): Newton saw the same in 1672. Turn: connection to history.
