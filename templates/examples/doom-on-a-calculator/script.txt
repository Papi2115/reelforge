Doom runs on almost anything. Fridges, watches, even a printer.

But this one is special: a school calculator on an exam bench, with only 61 KB of memory.

The original game needed four megabytes. Here, everything has to fit in 61 KB.

So hackers rewrote the engine, shrank the maps, and drew every frame in tiny pixels.

And there it is: demons and shotguns, on a calculator screen.

If it has a screen, it runs Doom.
